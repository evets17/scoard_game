# Cascadia — Base Game Scoring

In Cascadia, final scoring consists of wildlife scoring, habitat scoring, habitat majority bonuses, and unused nature tokens.

## 1. Wildlife scoring

Score each of the five wildlife species separately, using the corresponding scoring card selected at the beginning of the game.

| Wildlife | What determines points                          |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Bear     | Groups or pairs of bears, depending on the card |
| Elk      | Patterns and formations of connected elk        |
| Salmon   | Runs of connected salmon                        |
| Hawk     | Positioning and separation of hawks             |
| Fox      | Wildlife adjacent to foxes                      |

Each species has four possible scoring cards (A, B, C, D). Only the selected card for each species applies.

## 2. Habitat scoring

You score 1 point for each tile in your largest contiguous group of each habitat type.

The five habitats are Mountains, Forests, Prairies, Wetlands, and Rivers.

Important rules:

- Only matching habitat edges connect tiles into a habitat group.
- A tile showing two habitat types can contribute to both habitat groups.
- Score only the largest connected group for each habitat type, not every group.

## 3. Habitat majority bonuses

Compare each player's largest habitat group for each of the five habitat types.

| Players | Largest group | Second largest |
| ------- | ------------- | -------------- |
| 2       | 2 points      | 0              |
| 3–4     | 3 points      | 1 point        |

Ties split the points for the tied positions, rounded down.

## 4. Nature tokens

Each unused nature token is worth 1 point at the end of the game.

## 5. Winning and tiebreakers

Add all four scoring categories. The highest total wins.

If players tie, the player with the most unused nature tokens wins. If still tied, they share the victory.

A few important clarifications:

- In a 3–4 player game, two players tied for the largest habitat corridor each receive 2 bonus points; nobody receives second-place points.
- Three or four players tied for largest each receive 1 bonus point.
- Players tied for second place receive no bonus.
- In solo play, each habitat corridor of at least 7 tiles earns a 2-point bonus.

These are confirmed in the [Cascadia base-game rulebook, pages 8–9](https://meepletron-storage.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/resources/cascadia-rulebook.pdf).&#x20;
